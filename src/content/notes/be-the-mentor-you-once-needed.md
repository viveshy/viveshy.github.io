---
title: Be the Mentor You Once Needed
description: If someone genuinely wants to learn from you, make time for them.
pubDate: 2026-08-23
---

Show kindness and guidance to someone who seeks your mentorship. If
someone genuinely wants to learn from you, make time for them.

You might have been mentored by someone yourself. Think about that one
doubt they helped you clarify. That one suggestion they gave you. That one
direction they showed you when you didn't know what to do next. It might
have been a small effort for them, but it could have made a big difference
in your journey.

When you are in a position to mentor someone, take the time to do it. Your
small effort might change someone's life for the better.

I have been fortunate to be mentored by many people and to have had the
opportunity to work with some of them directly. I don't take that for
granted. And whenever someone seeks genuine guidance from me, I will
always try to make time for them.

If you have learned something along the way, don't keep it to yourself.
Pass it on.
